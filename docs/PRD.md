# Curais Product Requirements Document

## Product statement

Curais helps people act safely in a health moment, then keep the information needed for better follow-up care. SnapAid becomes the **Immediate Care** module inside Curais: fast, non-diagnostic first-aid guidance with direct emergency escalation.

## Problem and audience

People in India, particularly in Tier 2/3 cities, often lack a clear next step during a health incident and arrive at a clinic without an accessible longitudinal record. The initial users are patients/caregivers needing immediate guidance and small clinics needing a simple patient-record workflow.

## Product principles

- Safety before engagement: direct emergency escalation must remain visually dominant.
- Guidance, not diagnosis: never present an AI output as a confirmed condition.
- Consent and minimum data: collect only what is necessary for each capability.
- Mobile, low-bandwidth, and local-language first.

## Releases

### R1 — Curais Immediate Care (existing SnapAid, rebranded)

- Free-text symptom guidance and curated first-aid playbooks.
- Critical-vs-basic signs, emergency and poison-control calling.
- Safety disclaimer, local emergency-number detection, rate limiting, and feedback reporting.
- No account and no retained clinical history required.

### R2 — Personal Care Record

- Account creation, verified mobile number, profile, allergies, medications, conditions, and emergency contacts.
- User-controlled incident history: save, export, or delete a SnapAid/Curais guidance session.
- Consent screen before any health information is saved.

### R3 — Curais Clinic

- Clinic/staff workspace, patient lookup with explicit patient consent, visit notes, and structured vitals.
- Role-based access, audit trail, and patient sharing controls.
- Offline draft capture and safe conflict resolution; no silent overwrite.

### R4 — Intelligence and connected care

- Clinician-reviewed decision support, device/vitals ingestion, care reminders, and aggregate, de-identified operational insights.
- Emergency workflows and public-health integrations only after legal, clinical, and security validation.

## Success measures

| Area | Measure |
| --- | --- |
| Immediate care | guidance completion rate, emergency CTA visibility/click rate, harmful-output reports |
| Trust | consent completion, saved-record opt-in, deletion/export success |
| Clinic | weekly active clinics, visit documentation completion, median record-retrieval time |
| Safety | critical incidents, time to triage review, security/audit exceptions |

## Explicit non-goals for R1

- Diagnosis, prescriptions, or medication dosing generated by AI.
- Storing identifiable health data without an account and explicit consent.
- Autonomous emergency dispatch or clinical decision-making.
