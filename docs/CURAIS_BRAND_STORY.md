# The Curais Story

## A name built around care

**Curais** begins with *cura* - the Latin root for care, healing, and attention. It is paired with **AI**, not to make technology the hero, but to make care more available when people need clarity.

Curais means: **care, made more present by intelligence.**

## Why it matters

In a health moment, people do not need another complicated system. They need to know what to do now, who to call, and how to carry the right information into the next step of care. Curais starts there.

SnapAid becomes Curais Immediate Care: calm, actionable first-aid guidance at the moment of need. From that first moment, Curais can help people preserve the health context they choose to keep, share it safely with a clinician, and receive more continuous care over time.

## Brand promise

**Clear help now. Better care next.**

Curais is designed to feel capable but never clinical or cold; urgent when a situation is dangerous, and calm when people need reassurance. It respects the clinician's role, keeps people in control of their information, and treats AI as a carefully bounded support tool - never a substitute for professional care.

## Logo narrative

The preferred mark is a soft, open **C** that becomes a gentle pulse. The open form suggests an invitation to care, while the pulse represents both life and timely action. Its rounded geometry communicates warmth and accessibility; its precision reflects dependable health technology.

Use deep teal as the primary brand color for trust and steadiness. Reserve emergency red for critical calls to action only. This gives Curais a distinctive identity: calm by default, unmistakably urgent when it needs to be.
